My top 3 movies of all-time

Fast & Furious

Fast & Furious is a 2001 American action film directed by Rob Cohen and written by Gary Scott Thompson, Erik Bergquist, and David Ayer. It is the first installment in The Fast and the Furious franchise. The film stars Paul Walker as Brian O'Conner, a police officer who goes undercover to investigate the Los Angeles street racing scene led by Dominic Toretto (Vin Diesel). The film also features Michelle Rodriguez, Jordana Brewster, Rick Yune, Chad Lindberg, Johnny Strong, and Ted Levine in supporting roles.

Inception

Inception is a 2010 science fiction action film written and directed by Christopher Nolan, who also produced the film with Emma Thomas, his wife. The film stars Leonardo DiCaprio as a professional thief who steals information by infiltrating the subconscious of his targets. He is offered a chance to have his criminal history erased as payment for the implantation of another person's idea into a target's subconscious. The ensemble cast also includes Ken Watanabe, Joseph Gordon-Levitt, Marion Cotillard, Elliot Page, Tom Hardy, Cillian Murphy, Tom Berenger, Dileep Rao, and Michael Caine.

The Dark Knight

The Dark Knight is a 2008 superhero film directed, produced, and co-written by Christopher Nolan. Based on the DC Comics character Batman, the film is the second installment of Nolan's The Dark Knight Trilogy and a sequel to 2005's Batman Begins. It stars Christian Bale as Bruce Wayne / Batman, alongside Michael Caine, Heath Ledger, Gary Oldman, Aaron Eckhart, Maggie Gyllenhaal, and Morgan Freeman. In the film, Batman sets out to dismantle the remaining criminal organizations that plague Gotham City, but is menaced by an anarchistic mastermind known as the Joker (Ledger), who seeks to create chaos and force Batman closer to crossing the fine line between hero and vigilante.
